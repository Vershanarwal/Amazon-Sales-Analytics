# 🛒 Amazon Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Amazon Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze Amazon sales data and deliver meaningful business insights. It enables users to monitor sales performance, customer behavior, product categories, payment methods, and regional trends through interactive visualizations, helping support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance.
* Identify top-selling products and categories.
* Monitor monthly sales trends.
* Compare sales performance across states.
* Understand customer payment preferences.
* Track order status and fulfillment.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

* 💰 Total Sales KPI
* 📦 Total Orders KPI
* 🛒 Total Quantity Sold KPI
* 🚚 Total Shipping Cost KPI
* 💳 Average Order Value (AOV)
* 📈 Monthly Sales Trend Analysis
* 📊 Sales by Product Category
* 🏆 Top 10 Best-Selling Products
* 🌍 State-wise Sales Analysis
* 🥧 Payment Mode Distribution
* 📦 Order Status Analysis
* 🎛️ Interactive Slicers and Filters
